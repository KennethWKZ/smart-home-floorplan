LED strip supplies: wire to permanent power, not through the switch LED strip 火牛：接常电，不要经过 switch

The rule. Each LED strip's 24 V supply (火牛) is fed from permanent live and neutral, never from an H1 rocker's load terminal. That rocker is set to wireless (decoupled) mode in the Aqara app and switches the strip by sending a command to its driver. 规则：每条 LED strip 的 24V 火牛都接常电（live L + neutral N，不经过 switch），不可以接在 H1 switch 按键的 output（L1/L2/L3）。那个按键会在 Aqara App 里设成 wireless mode（decoupled），用 signal 叫 driver 开关灯条。

Why: each driver is also a Zigbee router. If its power is cut, nearby smart devices drop off the network. 原因：每个 driver 也是 Zigbee router，一断电，附近的 smart 设备会 offline。
